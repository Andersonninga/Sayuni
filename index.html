About Sayuni Child Reach Organization

Sayuni Child Reach Organization is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO), established with committed individuals in 2020 and registered 24/02/2025 (Reg No. 00NGO/R/8090) under the Tanzania NGO Act No. 24 of 2002.

Profile Organization

Sayuni Child Reach Organization is a Non Govermental Organization - NGO, established with committed person who had a one goal / Objective in 2020 and registered 24/02/2025 and it Obtained the registration number 00NGO/R/8090 under Tanzania NGO Act. No 24 of 2002.

Headquartered at P.O. Box 10252, Moivaro, Arusha, SCRO is dedicated to uplifting vulnerable children, empowering women and youth, promoting healthcare, conserving the environment, and building sustainable community capacity across Tanzania.

What We Do

Our Core Impact Programs

Holistic support structures designed to uplift children from early childhood through complete primary and secondary education.

Program 01

Education Support Program

Education is the ultimate tool to break generational poverty. SCRO currently supports **more than 70 children** to stay in school, excel academically, and complete their schooling journey.

  • Full payment of school tuition and mandatory examination fees.
  • Provision of official school uniforms, shoes, and sports gear.
  • Distribution of textbooks, exercise books, pens, and mathematical sets.
  • Extra weekend tuition classes and personalized academic mentorship.
Program 02

Health & Nutrition Program

A hungry or sick child cannot concentrate in class. Our health and nutrition initiatives ensure every child receives daily sustenance and medical care.

  • Daily school feeding initiatives providing hot, balanced nutritious meals.
  • Regular medical checks, deworming, and dental health assessments.
  • Hygiene, handwashing, and sanitation education in rural schools.
  • Nutrition awareness and food parcel support for vulnerable guardians.
Fund Meals for a Child
Program 03

Early Childhood Development

Laying a resilient cognitive and emotional foundation during the first critical years of a child's life.

  • Promoting early childhood education & kindergarten readiness.
  • Supporting physical, emotional, social, and cognitive milestones.
  • Creating safe, vibrant, and nurturing learning environments.
  • Training local caregivers and early learning educators.
